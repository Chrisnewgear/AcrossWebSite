# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Businesses and operations/logistics managers who need to move freight across borders — importers, exporters, and companies evaluating a freight-forwarding partner. They arrive to assess credibility fast: can this company reliably move my cargo, quote me, and let me track it? Context is a working evaluation, often desktop during business hours, sometimes mobile. Site is bilingual (Spanish / English) because the audience spans markets.

## Product Purpose

ACROSS is a freight-forwarding / logistics company. The marketing site exists to establish trust and convert visitors into leads: communicate global reach and service breadth (air, sea, land, customs), then drive the two primary actions — request a quote (Cotizar) and track a shipment (Rastreo) — plus contact. Success = a credible-enough impression that a business initiates a quote or contact.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, solid, corporate. Voice is confident and plain, not flashy. The brand should read as an established partner you can hand your cargo to — precision and reliability over spectacle. Color is committed (ACROSS blue #134289 + green #71C200) but disciplined; movement and scale carry energy without shouting.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI landing page**: eyebrow label above every section, identical icon+heading+text card grids, gradient text, template cadence.
- **Cheap / low-budget**: clashing color, cramped or inconsistent spacing, misalignment, clip-art.
- **Loud / cluttered**: too many competing accents, busy sections, overstimulating motion.

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility is the product.** Every detail either builds or erodes trust. Consistency and precision beat decoration.
2. **Move the visitor to one action per fold.** Quote / track / contact are the point; the design serves conversion, not ornament.
3. **Committed but disciplined color.** Blue and green carry the brand; neutrals stay honest (real contrast, no washed-out gray).
4. **Motion as reassurance, not spectacle.** Reveals and transitions are calm and purposeful; never the uniform fade-on-every-section reflex.
5. **Bilingual parity.** ES and EN must both read naturally and never break layout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast (the current muted grays need auditing against this). Visible keyboard focus on all interactive elements. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (already wired via the reveal system). Touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile.
